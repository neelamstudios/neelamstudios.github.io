Privacy Policy

Mahjong Moksha by Neelam Studios · Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Mahjong Moksha game ("the App") handles. Neelam Studios ("we", "us") built the App to be a calm, private experience.

Summary

  • We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information.
  • The App works offline. It does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the App saves a small file on your device containing: stages completed, Punya (in-game points), unlocked wisdom verses, the number of days you have opened the App, and your settings (such as music, sound, vibration and night mode). This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or choose "Reset all progress" in Settings.

Permissions

  • Vibration – used for gentle haptic feedback when you play. You can turn it off in Settings.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of the App does not show ads and does not include analytics or tracking tools. If a future version adds advertising (for example, optional reward videos) or analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released, describing what data those services collect and how you can control it.

Children

The App is designed for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
